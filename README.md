# AnimeFusion

> The public-facing AniFuze ecosystem and product experience.

AnimeFusion is the main customer-facing side of the AniFuze platform. It is designed as the professional product layer around the AniFuze system, covering the public website, services, packages, customer onboarding, and entry points into the platform.

## 🧩 Role in the ecosystem

AnimeFusion and AniFuze have intentionally different responsibilities:

- **AnimeFusion** — public website, product presentation, services, packages, customer-facing experience, and platform entry point.
- **AniFuze** — customer platform, backend/system, administration, provider infrastructure, and installation/runtime functionality.

## ✨ Product Areas

- Product and service presentation
- Packages and pricing
- Customer onboarding
- Authentication entry points
- Customer portal integration
- Marketplace and product ecosystem
- Support and service workflows
- Professional responsive UI

## 🚧 Status

AnimeFusion is under active development as the public/product layer of the AniFuze ecosystem.
